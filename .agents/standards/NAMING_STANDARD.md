# BAITHAK ERP – Naming Standard (NAMING_STANDARD.md)

## Conventions
- **Folders**: `kebab-case` (`menu-items/`, `payment-modes/`)
- **React Components**: `PascalCase.tsx` (`POSHeader.tsx`, `MenuItemFormDialog.tsx`)
- **API Files**: `camelCase.api.ts` (`menuItems.api.ts`, `categories.api.ts`)
- **Services**: `camelCase.service.ts` (`billing.service.ts`, `printer.service.ts`)
- **Stores**: `camelCase.store.ts` (`cart.store.ts`)
- **Hooks**: `useCamelCase.ts` (`useMenu.ts`, `useTables.ts`)
- **Schemas**: `camelCase.schema.ts` (`menuItem.schema.ts`)
