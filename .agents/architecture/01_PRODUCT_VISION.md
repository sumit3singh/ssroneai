# Product Vision Document (PVD) – BAITHAK ERP Charter #01

## 1. Product Vision
To build **THE BAITHAK (SSR INFINITY)** as the world's most powerful, metadata-driven, intuitive Enterprise Business Operating System for hospitality, food service, hotels, and retail chains.

## 2. Mission
Empower business owners to manage single or multi-branch enterprises seamlessly with real-time analytics, automated workflows, and absolute database integrity.

## 3. Target Industries
- Restaurants, Cafes, Quick Service Restaurants (QSR), Bar & Lounges
- Hotels, Resorts, Guest Houses, PG & Hostels
- Multi-Branch Food & Retail Chains

## 4. Multi-Tenant Architecture
Isolated multi-tenant data architecture where every tenant, company, and branch operates in complete data privacy.
